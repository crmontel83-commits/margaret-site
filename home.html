Meet Margaret

Margaret Feuerman writes epic fantasy about stubborn heroines, found family, and the people who stand between the world and the monsters. She shares her home—and most of her photo roll—with a very good dog and a lot of dice.

If you like sharp-edged magic, green-and-gold banners, and stories where hope hangs on the choices of one tired, determined girl, you’re in the right place.

You can find Margaret on Facebook and Instagram, or join the newsletter for bonus chapters and early extras.
