The Blessed Ladies

Holy Istana

Lady of Light • Yellow Topaz

Eldest Daughter of Amaya and Istvan, Holy Istana is the Lady of Light, and the Goddess of the Sun who gifts her faithful with the ability to call the Fire of Heaven. Froth cannot withstand its cleansing power, nor can most Frothlings. She is the Bright Lady, the Lady of Justice and Mercy, who sees the truth within one’s heart.

Upon the death of Istvan, the Father of Creation, at the hands of Vella, Istana took Istvan’s throne as the Queen of the Sacred Groves. She is the patroness of House Sonnenberg.

Shenesufa

Lady of Air • Gray Tourmaline

Shenesufa, the Silver Lady, Second Daughter of Amaya and Istvan, is the Daughter of Air. She is worshiped as the Lady of the Wind and Clouds, who grants her faithful detachment when emotions threaten to overwhelm.

As the Stormbringer, she is a ruthless, vengeful warrior who slips easily into violence. Her weapon is a double-headed spear named Elda-Barqa.
