The Blessed Ladies

The Blessed Ladies are honored through their gems, colors, symbols, and domains. More lore will be added here as Margaret expands the world of Keeper of the Gate.

Holy Istana

Yellow Topaz

Eldest Daughter of Amaya and Istvan, Holy Istana is the Lady of Light, and the Goddess of the Sun who gifts her faithful with the ability to call the Fire of Heaven. Froth cannot withstand its cleansing power, nor can most Frothlings. She is the Bright Lady, the Lady of Justice and Mercy, who sees the truth within one’s heart. Upon the death of Istvan, the Father of Creation, at the hands of Vella, Istana took Istvan’s throne as the Queen of the Sacred Groves. She is the patroness of House Sonnenberg. Holy Istana is usually depicted as a tall woman with dark skin and golden hair with hints of blue flame flickering through the strands. Clad in golden armor, she wields sword and spell against the Frothlings, alongside her husband, Sir Anstell, the Paladin who was brave enough to say “hello” and claim her heart.

Shenesufa

Gray Tourmaline

Shenesufa, the Silver Lady Second Daughter of Amaya and Istvan, Shenesufa is most like the mother. She understands souls and is the only one who knows into which body a soul is reborn. The Daughter of Air, she is worshiped as the Lady of the Wind and Clouds, who grants her faithful detachment when emotions threaten to overwhelm. As the Stormbringer, she is a ruthless, vengeful warrior who slips easily into violence. Cities burned when she hunted Vella after Amaya’s death. Her weapon is a double-headed spear named Elda-Barqa. Her faithful can call lightning from a cloudless sky to power their weapons. The storm within her is kept quiet through the love of her two consorts, Hertha and Gertrudis, the ancient Chieftains of the Heril tribe. House Heril claims Shenesufa as their patroness.
